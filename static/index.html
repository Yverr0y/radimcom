ACARS
ACARSCPDLCADS-C

Classic ACARS on the VHF airband (AM, 2400 bit/s). Works in any mode: the decoder demodulates AM itself. The buttons tune the European ACARS channels; 131.725 MHz is the busiest. VDL Mode 2 (digital, 31.5 kbit/s): the VFO runs at 2.1 MS/s around 136.850 MHz and all European VDL2 channels (136.725–136.975 MHz) are decoded at once; the button re-centres the VFO. Most airline ACARS over Europe uses VDL2, and European ATN CPDLC exists only here. Tags: ACARS plain ACARS message, CPDLC controller–pilot messages (↑ from ATC, ↓ from the aircraft; FANS in ACARS, or the European ATN CPDLC on VDL2), ADS-C automatic position reports (FANS in ACARS, or ATN on VDL2). Click a tag at the top of the card to show only those messages (CPDLC = FANS and ATN CPDLC). MIN / MRN = message number / number of the message it answers. ✗ = CRC error. Blue = aircraft type, flight level and speed looked up on ADS-B (adsb.fi / adsb.lol, needs internet); click the callsign or flight to open the map. Country = from the aircraft's ICAO address.

WEFAX

Format (IOC from the start tone, LPM, line start from phasing) and fine tuning (AFC ±3 kHz) are detected automatically. Finished images are saved to the data\wefax folder. Click in the image = that spot becomes the left edge. Slanted image: ALIGN, then click two points on a line that should be vertical.

Waiting for a signal… Tune a WEFAX station (e.g. DWD), the image starts by itself.
DAB+

DAB / DAB+ digital radio in Band III (174–240 MHz), decoded by welle-cli (welle.io). The VFO runs at 2.048 MS/s on the centre of the chosen channel; pick a station below. Audio comes through MAIN volume (WAV recording does not include DAB). RTL-SDR only. Channels of your region: e.g. Slovakia / Czechia 12C, 12D, 7D, Austria 5C, 5D, 8B, 11C.

SSTV

Decodes the demodulated audio (USB/LSB on HF, FM e.g. ISS 145.800). Mode and start from the VIS code; without VIS the image starts from the line sync pulses. Fine tuning (AFC) and slant are corrected automatically. Use a filter of at least 2.7 kHz. Finished images are saved to the data\sstv folder.

Waiting for a signal… The image starts by itself on the VIS code.

SET · Settings
Settings for

Each VFO keeps its own settings. Dongles and VFO assignment are in data\vfos.json.

Dongle

Switching closes the dongle and opens the selected one. Each dongle remembers its own upconverter, LO and ppm.

Upconverter

Auto: below 30 MHz via the converter, VHF/UHF direct.

Above 30 MHz the signal must go straight to the dongle (bypass switch on the converter or another antenna).

Squelch

AM, FM and WFM each keep their own level; the slider sets the one for the current mode. Audio is muted while the signal is below the level (yellow mark on the S-meter). Far left = off. AM (airband): the carrier opens the squelch, so set it just above the noise level.

AFI – inversion frequency (Hz)

Audio frequency inversion (AFI key): the audio spectrum is mirrored around this frequency (f → 2·F − f, band 0 … 2·F). The same setting scrambles and descrambles. Default 1750 Hz (band up to 3500 Hz). 500–4000 Hz.

Tuner gain

Digital gain in the RTL2832 after the A/D converter, independent of the tuner gain. Helps weak signals at low tuner gain, but can overload with strong ones.

Display width

Only if the upconverter or LNA is powered by the dongle through the antenna connector.
