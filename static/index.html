RTTY

Baudot (ITA2). Shift, speed and polarity are detected automatically from the two tones in the filter passband (any mode; use USB/LSB with a filter wide enough for both tones). Mark = the higher RF tone (normal). E.g. amateur RTTY 45.45 Bd / 170 Hz, DWD weather 50 Bd / 450 Hz.

FT8 decoder

Decodes the USB passband 0–3 kHz above the tuned frequency in 15 s cycles timed by the PC clock (must be accurate to ±1 s).

ACARS

Classic ACARS on the VHF airband (AM, 2400 bit/s). Works in any mode: the decoder demodulates AM itself. The buttons tune the European ACARS channels; 131.725 MHz is the busiest. VDL Mode 2 (digital, 31.5 kbit/s): the VFO runs at 2.1 MS/s around 136.850 MHz and all European VDL2 channels (136.725–136.975 MHz) are decoded at once; the button re-centres the VFO. Most airline ACARS over Europe uses VDL2, and European ATN CPDLC exists only here. Tags: ACARS plain ACARS message, CPDLC controller–pilot messages (↑ from ATC, ↓ from the aircraft), ADS-C automatic position reports, ATN CPDLC European CPDLC (VDL2 only). MIN / MRN = message number / number of the message it answers. ✗ = CRC error. Blue = aircraft type and position looked up on ADS-B (adsb.fi / adsb.lol, needs internet); click the registration or flight to open the map.

WEFAX

Format (IOC from the start tone, LPM, line start from phasing) and fine tuning (AFC ±3 kHz) are detected automatically. Finished images are saved to the data\wefax folder. Click in the image = that spot becomes the left edge.

Waiting for a signal… Tune a WEFAX station (e.g. DWD), the image starts by itself.
SSTV

Decodes the demodulated audio (USB/LSB on HF, FM e.g. ISS 145.800). Mode and start from the VIS code; without VIS the image starts from the line sync pulses. Fine tuning (AFC) and slant are corrected automatically. Use a filter of at least 2.7 kHz. Finished images are saved to the data\sstv folder.

Waiting for a signal… The image starts by itself on the VIS code.

SET · Settings
Settings for

Each VFO keeps its own settings. Dongles and VFO assignment are in data\vfos.json.

Dongle

Switching closes the dongle and opens the selected one. Each dongle remembers its own upconverter, LO and ppm.

Upconverter

Auto: below 30 MHz via the converter, VHF/UHF direct.

Above 30 MHz the signal must go straight to the dongle (bypass switch on the converter or another antenna).

Squelch

AM, FM and WFM each keep their own level; the slider sets the one for the current mode. Audio is muted while the signal is below the level (yellow mark on the S-meter). Far left = off. AM (airband): the carrier opens the squelch, so set it just above the noise level.

Tuner gain

Digital gain in the RTL2832 after the A/D converter, independent of the tuner gain. Helps weak signals at low tuner gain, but can overload with strong ones.

Display width
