ISM decoder (lib\ism)
=====================

RADIMCOM decodes devices on 433 and 868 MHz with rtl_433, which runs as a separate program. It connects to a small
rtl_tcp server in RADIMCOM (127.0.0.1) for the raw I/Q samples of the VFO and writes the decoded messages (JSON) to its
standard output.
