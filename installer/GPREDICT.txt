Gpredict (apps\gpredict)
========================

Gpredict does the satellite tracking and Doppler correction. It runs as a separate program and talks to RADIMCOM over
TCP on this PC: a rotator on port 4533 and a radio on port 4532. The GP key on the page opens and closes it.

RADIMCOM cannot use this folder under a folder named "lib": Gpredict would look for its data in the wrong place and exit.

Programs and libraries in this folder:

- gpredict.exe, gpredict-con.exe, libsgpsdp.dll: Gpredict 2.7, Copyright (c) Alexandru Csete OZ9AEC and contributors,
  GNU General Public License v2 or later (doc\COPYING). Source: https://github.com/csete/gpredict (release v2.7).
  This is a MODIFIED version. Changes made for RADIMCOM in November 2026, in installer\gpredict_radimcom.patch:
  - src/gtk-rig-ctrl.c, src/gtk-rot-ctrl.c: strcasestr replaced by gpredict_strcasestr, Windows does not have strcasestr.
  - src/gtk-rig-ctrl.c, src/gtk-rig-ctrl.h: sends the selected satellite and transponder to the radio server
    ("\set_trsp satellite|transponder"). Other radio servers refuse the command and nothing else changes.
  - src/gtk-rig-ctrl.c, src/gtk-rig-ctrl.h, src/gtk-rot-ctrl.c, src/gtk-rot-ctrl.h: send the time to the next AOS or LOS to the radio and
    rotator servers ("\set_pass satellite|AOS|seconds"), and the wanted antenna position to the rotator server ("\set_aim az el").
    Other servers refuse these commands, and they are not sent again on that connection.
  - src/gtk-rot-ctrl.c: stops a second timer that stayed running and crashed Gpredict after the rotator window was closed.
  - src/first-time.c: creates the radio RADIMCOM.rig and the rotator RADIMCOM.rot when they do not exist.
- GTK 3, GLib, GDK-PixBuf, Pango, Cairo, ATK, FriBidi, Graphite2, libdatrie, libthai, librsvg, libiconv, gettext
  (libintl), xz: LGPL. libjbig (jbigkit): GPL v2. Adwaita icons: LGPL v3 or CC BY-SA 3.0.
- FreeType, zstd, HarfBuzz, Fontconfig, Expat, libepoxy, libffi, libpng, libjpeg-turbo, libtiff, libwebp, libdeflate,
  Lerc, brotli, bzip2, libxml2, PCRE2, pixman, zlib, winpthreads: permissive licences (FreeType under the FTL, zstd under BSD).
- GCC runtime libraries: GPL with the GCC Runtime Library Exception.
- All of these are MSYS2 UCRT64 packages, sources at https://github.com/msys2/MINGW-packages

RADIMCOM_sources_v<version>.zip on the release page has the Gpredict source, the patch and the sources of the
GPL and LGPL libraries.

How it was built (MSYS2, "UCRT64" environment):

  installer\build_gpredict.sh does all of it: downloads the release, applies the patch, compiles with win32\Makefile
  and copies the program with the DLLs it imports to apps\gpredict.
